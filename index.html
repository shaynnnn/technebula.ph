About Us

TechNebula is a cooperative focused on smart tech for agriculture. We design innovative tools like our automated humidifier to help mushroom farmers improve efficiency, reduce labor, and boost production.

The Future of Farming Starts Here

TechNebula’s Mushroom Humidifier was developed to help mushroom growers overcome one of the most critical challenges in cultivation: maintaining optimal humidity. Manual misting is time-consuming and inconsistent—our product eliminates that problem with a smart, automated system that works 24/7.

It provides consistent humidity, sends alerts when water needs refilling, and requires little to no supervision—perfect for modern farming.

Why Mushroom Farmers Need This

  • Traditional humidifiers require constant adjustment
  • Inconsistent humidity leads to low yield or failed crops
  • Farmers lose income due to environmental instability
  • No affordable tech tailored for mushroom farming… until now

THE SOLUTION: TECHNEBULA’S MUSHROOM HUMIDIFIER

  • 🌀 Auto-adjusts humidity to keep it between 85–95%
  • 🔔 Notifies the user when water is low via mobile integration
  • 📱 Real-time LCD display for temperature and humidity readings
  • ⚙️ Low-cost and durable design built for local farmers
  • 🌱 Eco-conscious materials and energy-efficient operation
